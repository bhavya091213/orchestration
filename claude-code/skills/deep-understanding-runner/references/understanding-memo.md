# Understanding Memo

Use this format for deep dives that will become durable project knowledge.

```markdown
# Understanding: [Domain]

## One-Screen Summary
[The shortest useful explanation of the domain.]

## Concept Map
| Concept | Role | Related Concepts |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Workflow
1. [step]
2. [step]

## Invariants
- [thing that must remain true]

## Failure Modes
| Failure | Cause | Detection | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Sources
- [path/url] - [what it supports]

## Open Questions
- [question] - [owner/source to check]
```

Keep it short enough to paste into a future handoff.
